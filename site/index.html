The 3D waypoint arrow for WoW Forever

Waypoint Tracker is a free addon for World of Warcraft: Forever. Set a waypoint with /way 42 65, the world map or a name, and a 3D arrow over your character points the way, showing the distance and time to arrive. It turns from gold to green as you get close and clears itself when you arrive.

Features

An arrow that shows how close you are

Gold far away, green as you get there, with the name, distance and time to arrive underneath. It never catches mouse clicks, so right-click to attack always works.

Set a waypoint your way

A small window, /way coordinates, a quest or place name, the Find window, or Ctrl + right-click on the world map. Pasted coordinates like 45.2 67.8 split into X and Y by themselves.

Share spots in chat

Send any spot, or where you stand, to Party, Raid, Guild, Say or a whisper as a clickable map pin. It works for friends without the addon too.

Arrive and move on

A sound plays, the waypoint clears and the arrow points to the next closest one. When you die it points to your corpse, then back to your waypoint.

Simple by default

Four basic settings. Colour styles, fading, hiding in combat or on flight paths, map and minimap pins, metres and more are behind Show more options.

Moves with Edit Mode

Press Esc > Edit Mode to drag the arrow and its text, set their size and visibility, and keep a spot per layout.

Find quests, NPCs, enemies, objects and items

Press Quests, NPCs, Enemies or Objects in the window, or type /wp find hogger. Search as you type, then double-click a result and the arrow points to the closest spot. Quests show who gives them, where the objectives are and where to hand them in. Items show what drops or sells them. One click finds the nearest mailbox, innkeeper, flight master, repair, bank or auction house.

Find covers the classic world and WoW Forever's new zones (Mount Hyjal, Riverglades, Zephras Isle, Shen'dralas), including about 3,000 quests and 6,600 items beyond the classic world, and every item, flight path and town in the WoW Forever game files in nine languages. It learns while you play: the NPCs and enemies around you with their titles (search blacksmith to find every blacksmith you've seen), what vendors sell, quest givers and what drops from what you loot. What you see in the game always wins.

What it learns stays on your computer and keeps only what's new. Found a spot that's off? Press Wrong spot? Correct it. To help everyone, press Share discoveries and paste the text into a GitHub issue; what's new goes into a coming update.

How to install Waypoint Tracker

  1. CurseForge app: choose World of Warcraft, then the Forever game version, search for Waypoint Tracker and click Install.
  2. Wago: install it from addons.wago.io.
  3. By hand: download WaypointTracker-v@VERSION@.zip from GitHub Releases and unzip it into Interface\AddOns in your WoW Forever folder (during the beta: World of Warcraft\_classic_beta_\Interface\AddOns). You get two folders, WaypointTracker and WaypointTracker_Data.

Then start the game, or type /reload, and type /wp.

Chat commands

/wpOpen or close the window
/way 42 65Waypoint in your current zone
/way Elwynn Forest 42 65 GoldshireWaypoint in any zone, with a name. Part of a zone name is enough, in any client language.
/way hoggerGo straight to something by name, or search Find
/way #37 42 65Waypoint by map ID, for guide writers
/wp here or /waybWaypoint where you're standing
/wp find [name]Search quests, NPCs, enemies, objects and items
/wp share [zone X Y]Share a spot, or where you are, in chat
/wp closest or /cwayPoint to the closest waypoint
/wp clearRemove the arrow's waypoint (/wp clear all removes all)
/wp listList your waypoints with distances
/wp arrowShow or hide the arrow
/wp helpList every command in the game

Key bindings are under Key Bindings > AddOns, and the settings under Options > AddOns > Waypoint Tracker.

Questions

How do I set a waypoint in WoW Forever?

Install Waypoint Tracker, then type /way 42 65 in chat for your current zone, or /way Westfall 56.3 47.1 Sentinel Hill for another zone. You can also type /wp to open the window, hold Ctrl and right-click the world map, or search a quest, NPC or object by name with /wp find. A 3D arrow then points the way.

How do I find an NPC, quest giver or vendor in WoW Forever?

Type /wp find and a name (or the title of an NPC you have seen, like blacksmith), or press NPCs in the Waypoint Tracker window. Double-click a result and the arrow points to the closest spot. Find covers the classic world and WoW Forever's new zones, quests and items, and adds the NPCs you see while you play.

How do I install addons for WoW Forever?

Use the CurseForge app (World of Warcraft, Forever) or Wago, or download the zip from GitHub and unzip it into Interface\AddOns in your WoW Forever folder. During the beta that is World of Warcraft\_classic_beta_\Interface\AddOns. Waypoint Tracker gives you two folders: WaypointTracker and WaypointTracker_Data.

Is Waypoint Tracker free?

Yes. It is free and open source under the MIT licence, with readable code, no ads and nothing to buy.

Does Waypoint Tracker send my data anywhere?

No. What the addon learns while you play stays in its saved file on your computer. You share it only if you choose to: press Share discoveries in Find and paste the text into a GitHub issue.

Which languages does Waypoint Tracker support?

Nine: English, German, French, Spanish, Portuguese, Russian, Korean, Simplified Chinese and Traditional Chinese. It follows your game client's language, and zone names in /way work in every language.

Can other addons send waypoints to Waypoint Tracker?

Yes. Quest guides, treasure maps and other addons that send waypoints to an arrow send them straight to Waypoint Tracker, with nothing to set up.
