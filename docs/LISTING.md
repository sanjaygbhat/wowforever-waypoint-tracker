## Summary (CurseForge and Wago)

```text
A 3D waypoint arrow for WoW Forever: /way coordinates, find any quest, NPC or item, share map pins, treasure alerts (beta). TomTom-compatible.
```

The release brings one window with Waypoints, Find and Routes tabs. Real routes (beta) adds travel guidance through passes and gates and by transport; keep it a supporting feature.
